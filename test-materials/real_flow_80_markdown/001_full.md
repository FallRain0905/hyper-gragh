# EARLY-CAREERRESEARCHERAWARD

Ya You - winner.Dr. Ya You is a Professor at the Wuhan University of Technology(WUT）in China.Her research interest focuses on electrochemical energy storage and conversion technology, with the main emphasis on developing advanced materials for batteries operated under extreme conditions. She earned her Ph.D.degree from the Institute of Chemistry, Chinese Academy of Sciences (ICCAS),in 2015. Followed by postdoc research at the University of Texas at Austin, she joined WUT as a full professor in 2019. She has published over 60 papers with more than 90oo citations and an h-index of 41. She has filed more than 10 patents and patent applications,with some of these breakthroughs having great potential to be translated into commercialization toward electrical transportation and grid energy storage.She has also received numerous awards throughout her career, such as “TR35 Asia Pacific".
