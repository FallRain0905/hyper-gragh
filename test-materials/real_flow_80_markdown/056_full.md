The advancement of neutral aqueous organic redox flow batteries (AORFBs) is critically constrained by the insuficient multi-state stability of organic electrolyte materials,particularly under prolonged redox process. Through imidazole-driven multi-hydrogen bonding engineering strategy,this work developed a series of naphthalene diimide featuring exceptional solubility $( 1 . 4 3 ~ \mathrm { { ~ M } ) }$ and configurational stability (NICS: $\cdot 1 8 . 5 4 4 $ -14.57).Molecular dynamics simulations (MD)combined with experimental characterization quantitatively validate the synergistic hydrogen-bond network involving (i) directional carbonyl groups,(ij imidazole moieties and (i) terminal hydroxyl across the NDI core and alkyl chains.Paired with imidazolium-functionalized nitroxide radical catholytes, the $^ { 1 \mathrm { ~ M ~ } }$ battery demonstrates unprecedented cyclability- $9 9 . 9 3 5 5 \%$ daily capacity retention over 7OO cycles (27 days).This work establishes a molecular engineering paradigm where imidazoleorchestrated hierarchical hydrogen-bonding architecture overcomes the inherent solubility-stability trade-off of naphthalene diimide materials in AORFBs.

# 1．Introduction

Safe and efficient energy storage solutions have emerged as pressing global challenges,attracting profound attention from all sectors of society[1-3].To address this challenge,researchers have explored and developed various storage technologies, encompassing two major categories: physical storage and electrochemical storage [4].Physical storage methods,such aspumped-storage hydroelectricity [5] and compressed-air energy storage [6],though possessing unique advantages,are constrained by geographical location and environmental conditions,limiting their widespread global application.In contrast, battery energy storage [7-9],as a representative of electrochemical storage,has garnered significant attention due to its vast potential for application. however, the advancement of large-scale energy storage utilizing traditional batteries is constrained by safety considerations [10, 11].These characteristic positions flow batteries as exceptionally valuable in applications where high safety is paramount [12-16].Against this backdrop,neutral aqueous organic redox flow batteries (AORFBs) have emerged as strong contenders for the next generation of commercial flow batteries,thanks to their neutral electrolyte environment and high energy density [17-19].

Neutral AORFBs use electrolyte materials composed of small organic molecules.These materials possess characteristics such as easy synthesis,low cost,and multi-electron transfer [19-21].Currently，the research on catholyte materials includes nitroxide radicals [22-24] and ferrocenes [25,26],while the anolyte materials include quinones [27, 28],ketones [29,30],viologens [31-35], phenazines [36-38],arylene diimides [39-41],and so on, these materials are all potential candidates for commercialization.However,there are still challenges such as insuficient structural stability during the two-electron energy storage.

Arylene diimide derivatives constitute a class of materials distinguished by their two-electron transfer characteristics, stable structures, rapid electron transfer,exceptional thermal stability,and ease of modification. They are commonly applied in fields such as batteries [42],molecular sensors [43,44],medicinal application [45],organic semiconductors [46,47],and optoelectronics [48].Additionally,it has made significant research advancements in the chemistry field, particularly in supramolecular assemblies and molecular switching devices [49,50].

Recently,naphthalene diimide derivatives,as an important class of arylene diimides,have been successfully introduced into the field of neutral AORFBs.The two redox properties of naphthalene diimide derivatives enable the implementation of a two-electron storage mechanism in battery applications,which can increase the theoretical capacity by $1 0 0 \%$ compared to traditional one-electron storage systems. Through quaternary ammonium salt modification technology，the research team successfully synthesized modified arylene diimide materials such as NDI,PDI,and TPDI,which exhibited excellent battery performance [39].Furthermore, the team also published a novel class of naphthalene diimide materials (siol-NDI,diol-NDI and dex-NDI)

However,the current methods for modifying the water solubility of the side chains of imide materials remain relatively limited,primarily confined to the introduction of alkyl chains.This limitation results in a relatively scarce variety and quantity of hydrogen bonds formed between the side chains and solvents.Imidazole,as an aromatic compound witha five-membered ring structure and significant electronwithdrawing capability,exhibits broad application potential in various fields such as ionic liquids,electrochromic technology,and flow batteries [23,54,55]. # 3.3.Density functional theory calculations (DFT)

The optical characteristics of the three molecules were examined using UV/Vis spectroscopy (Fig. S15).Notably,their absorption peaks coincide at $3 6 2 ~ \mathrm { n m }$ and $3 8 1 ~ \mathrm { { n m } }$ .Moreover, the highest occupied molecular orbital (HOMO) to lowest unoccupied molecular orbital (LUMO) energy gaps for these molecules were determined to be $3 . 1 9 \mathrm { e V }$ through the Tauc plot method [6O]. Complementing the experimental analysis, Density Functional Theory (DFT）was employed to theoretically compute the properties of the three molecules.The theoretical

UV/Vis absorption spectra for their oxidized and reduced states were calculated and correlated well with the experimental spectroscopic data (Figs. S16-S18).Consistent with the experimental values,the calculated HOMO-LUMO energy gap for all three molecules was found to be 3.54 eV(Fig.2a).Additionally,an intriguing trend emerged where the LUMO energy level of the oxidized state increased with the elongation of the carbon chain.This shift in the first oxidation potential towards the negative direction implies that an extended carbon chain length leads to a lower potential, aligning with literature findings [59].The structural diagrams of the oxidized and reduced states of the molecules are depicted in Fig.2b.Nuclear Independent Chemical Shifts (NICS) were computed within the molecular rings to verify their aromatic properties. In their oxidized states,the NICS(1) values for both ring A and ring B of the three molecules exhibited positive values, indicative of nonaromatic characteristics.Conversely,upon electron gain,all NICS(1） values within the rings transitioned to negative,signifying the attainment of stable aromatic configurations.Additionally,in both the oxidized and reduced states,the overall NICs(1) values for the naphthalene rings of NDI- $\mathbf { \cdot } \mathbf { C _ { 3 } }$ -MzOH remained negative,with minimal variation in aromaticity (-18.544 to -14.577).This further substantiates the conjugation and uniform electron distribution across the naphthalene rings,thereby demonstrating the stability of the naphthalene diimide molecules.

By calculating the electrostatic potentials,we conducted an in-depth investigation into the electron cloud distribution of imidazoliumfunctionalized naphthalene diimides during electrochemical processes (Figs.S19-S21).The research results reveal that the derivative exhibits an overall electron-deficient state in its oxidized form,with the imidazole ring displaying a more prominent electron-deficient character compared to the naphthalene ring.This indicates the electronwithdrawing effect of the imidazole ring. Upon acquiring electrons, the naphthalene ring exhibits a higher electron-rich property.Furthermore,compared to naphthalene diimides linked with three carbon atoms,those linked with only two carbon atoms exhibit more prominent electron-withdrawing properties of the imidazole ring and a more uniform electron cloud distribution,this result indicates that 1 has better chemical stability than 2 and 3.In the radical state,we further confirmed through calculations of electron spin density that the radical electrons are primarily distributed over the naphthalene ring (Fig.S22). Additionally,we conducted a comparative analysis of the Mulliken charges of naphthalene diimides modified with quaternary ammonium salts and imidazole (Figs.S23-S26).Due to the relatively weaker electron-withdrawing ability of imidazole compared to quaternary ammonium salts,the naphthalene rings in compounds 1,2,and 3 exhibit a more balanced charge distribution,which stabilizes their radical and reduced states.Furthermore,the volumes of the three molecules were calculated to be 551.96 $\mathring { \mathbf { A } } ^ { 3 }$ ， $5 9 8 . 5 6 \mathrm { ~ \AA ^ { 3 } }$ ，and $6 6 8 . 1 5 \mathrm { ~ \AA ~ } ^ { 3 }$ ， respectively.A larger molecular volume is advantageous in preventing molecular crossover, thereby enhancing battery longevity.Collectively, these test results underscore the incorporation of imidazole enhances the chemical stability of imide-based materials.

# 4.Conclusion

In conclusion,a series of imidazolium-functionalized naphthalene diimides (NDI- $\mathbf { \delta C _ { 2 } }$ -MzMe,NDI- $\mathbf { \cdot c _ { 3 } }$ -MzMe,NDI ${ \bf \cdot C _ { 3 } }$ -MzOH）exhibiting high solubility and stable electrochemical properties have been reported as promising anolyte materials for AORFBs.The battery's stability is augmented via stacking effects and the formation of imidazole-induced hydrogen bonding.This enhancement is substantiated through a combination of MD (Molecular Dynamics） simulations and experimental validations,which confirm the synergistic action of multiple hydrogen bonds.The electrochemical stability of the molecules was further validated through sampling and analysis of redox potentials during batery cycling,along with NMR data.In situ pH measurements confirmed that the electrolyte remained neutral throughout the entire cycling process. The1 M NDI $\mathbf { C _ { 3 } }$ -MzMe/MiAcNH-TEMPO-based AORFB retained acapacity of $7 2 . 6 4 6 8 \%$ with a daily retention rate of $9 8 . 8 6 0 3 \%$ over 620 cycles (24 days).Even more impressive,the 1 M NDI- ${ \bf \cdot } { \bf c _ { 3 } }$ -MzOH/ MiAcNH-TEMPO-based AORFB demonstrated remarkable stability, maintaining a capacity of $8 0 . 4 8 0 9 \%$ after 1000 cycles (40 days),with a dailyretention rate of $9 9 . 5 1 2 1 \%$ .These findings underscore the significantimprovementsin battery performance exhibited by the imidazolium-functionalized naphthalene diimides,positioning them as formidable competitors among commercialized materials for organic flow batteries.
