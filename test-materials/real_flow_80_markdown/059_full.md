Iron-chromiumredox flow batteries (ICRFBs)are highly promising forlarge-scale energy storage.However,their performance is limited by sluggish reaction kinetics,particularly for the $\mathrm { C r } ^ { 3 + } / \mathrm { C r } ^ { 2 + }$ redox couple,and the competing hydrogen evolution reaction (HER).In this study,we enhanced the electrochemical performance of carbon cloth (CC)electrodes via a one-step nitrogen and phosphorus co-doping strategy. By precisely controlling thedopant concentration,we introduced tailored NandPfunctional groups,which increased the specific surface area and improved hydrophilicity.Synchrotron radiation analysis and diferential electrochemical mas spectrometry demonstrated that the optimized electrode framework significantly enhances the kinetics of the $\mathrm { C r } ^ { 3 + } /$ $\mathrm { C r } ^ { 2 + }$ redox couple and effectively suppresss HER.Furthermore,electron density difference and reaction pathway analyses revealed that the N-Pco-doped structure promotes an eficient surface electronic configuration, facilitates reactant adsorption, inhibits $\mathrm { H } ^ { + }$ migration,and reduces competitive electron capture. Benefiting from the improved $\mathrm { C r } ^ { 3 + } / \mathrm { C r } ^ { \bar { 2 } + }$ reaction kinetics,the ICRFB single battery equipped with the optimized $0 . 1 5 ~ \mathrm { M - N P / C C }$ electrode achieved a high energy efficiency (EE) of $7 7 \ \%$ at $2 0 0 \mathrm { \ m A \ c m ^ { - 2 } }$ and maintained stable performance over 3Oocycles.This work highlights the potentialof multi-element co-doping for developing highperformance flow battery electrodes and elucidates the critical role of defect modulation in enhancing redox kinetics.

# 1．Introduction

The rapid global growth of renewable energy sources has positioned energy storage technologies as key enablers for their large-scale adoption.(Fan et al., 2024; Gautam et al., 2023; Jiang et al. (2024); Liu et al., 2023; Olabi et al., 2023;Weber et al., 2011) Among electrochemical energy storage systems,the iron-chromium redox flow battery (ICRFB) is a highly promising candidate for large-scale,long-duration storage due to its low cost, scalability,and inherent safety.(Sun and Zhang, 2022; Wang et al., 202l;Waters et al., 202O) However, the practical application of ICRFBs is hindered by the low reactivity of carbon-based electrodes and the sluggish kinetics of the $\mathrm { C r } ^ { 3 + } / \mathrm { C r } ^ { \mathrm { \bar { 2 } + } }$ redox couple. These limitations exacerbate the competing hydrogen evolution reaction (HER)at the anode,leading to irreversible capacity loss,especially under high current densities and long-term cycling.(Ahn et al., 2021;

Wan et al.，2O23) Therefore,developing electrode materials with a larger specific surface area,abundant active sites,and the ability to suppress HER—while maintaining acid and corrosion resistance-is crucial.(Huo et al., 2024; Qian et al., 2023; Wei et al., 2017).

Significant research efforts have been dedicated to modifying carbon-based electrodes for ICRFBs.(Gencten and Sahin, 2O2O; Sun and Zhang,2O22) Carbon cloth (CC),in particular,has attracted attention due to its excellent chemical stability and corrosion resistance.(Niu et al. (2024)) However,its limited specific surface area and low intrinsic electrochemical activity result in poor $\mathrm { C r } ^ { 3 + } / \mathrm { C r } ^ { 2 + }$ redox kinetics, making it susceptible to competing with $\mathrm { H } ^ { + }$ for electrons and thus promoting HER.(Xu et al.,2O23) Various modification strategies, including heat treatment,(Reynard et al., 2018; Sun et al., 2021) catalyst deposition, (He et al., 2022; Niu et al. (2024)) and the introduction of functional groups,(Jiang et al. (2024); Liao et al., 2024; Qi et al., 2023) have been explored to enhance electrocatalytic activity.Heat treatment introduces oxygen-containing functional groups and defect sites,improving the wettability of carbon materials and facilitating the adsorption of Cr and Fe ions.(Niu et al.(2O24)) Heteroatom doping can create new active sites and increase the specific surface area,accelerating charge transfer and providing more reaction sites.Nevertheless,the defect sites introduced by single-atom doping often inadvertently exacerbate HER.(Wu et al., 2024).

Nitrogen (N) is a commonly used dopant. It is widely accepted that N doping modifies the Fermi level of carbon materials by introducing active sites such as pyridinic-N and pyrrolic-N,thereby enhancing the conductivity and electrochemical activity for the $\mathrm { C r } ^ { 3 + } / \mathrm { C r } ^ { 2 + }$ couple. However,N doping can also disrupt the graphene structure and promote HER.(Xu et al.,2024) In contrast, phosphorus (P) doping,owing to its larger atomic radius and lone pair of electrons,induces charge redistribution on the electrode surface.This can modulate proton adsorption sites and the surface charge distribution,potentially reducing the driving force for HER.(Guo et al., 2O11) For example, Jang et al.(Jang et al.,2024) developed a high-performance composite electrode (NCC) by growing nitrogen-doped carbon nanofibers (N-doped CNFs) on carbon cloth,enhancing both hydrophilicity and vanadium ion redox activity through defect and electronic structure regulation.Zhang et al. (Zhang et al.,2O22) fabricated an N,P co-doped/reduced graphene oxide (rGO)-modified melamine foam-derived carbon electrode (NP-CMF-G-1ooo),achieving synergistic enhancement of electrode structure and catalytic activity was achieved through a low-cost impregnationcarbonization process.Despite these advances,heteroatom doping often introduces non-linear defects,limiting further performance improvements.Additionally,the variety of precursors used makes precise stoichiometric control challenging，frequently leading to non-uniform dopant distribution.Therefore,developing simple and efficient methods to produce high-performance electrodes that simultaneously suppress HER remains a critical need.(Park et al., 2021).

In this work,we propose an N-P co-doping strategy to address the HER issue in ICRFBs.While traditional N-doping introduces beneficial active sites,it can compromise the graphene structure and promote HER.We hypothesize that the additional introduction of P atoms,due to their distinct electronic properties,will reshape the surface charge distribution and reconstructa stable framework,thereby lowering the HER driving force.We selected ammonium dihydrogen phosphate (ADP) as a single source for both N and P due to its low decomposition temperature and strong etching capability,which helps reduce process cost. The resulting unique co-doped graphene skeleton is designed to inhibit $\mathrm { H } ^ { + }$ migration and surface HER.This combination of high electrochemical activity and effective HER suppression was verified through density functional theory (DFT) calculations and experimental characterization. The ICRFB equipped with the optimized 0.15 M-NP/CC electrode achieved an energy efficiency of $7 7 \ \%$ at $2 0 0 \mathrm { \ m A \ c m ^ { - 2 } }$ without any additional catalyst.Furthermore,it demonstrated stable operation over 300 cycles at $1 4 0 \mathrm { \ m A \ c m ^ { - 2 } }$ ，with a maximum EE of $8 3 . 4 7 ~ \%$ and an average EE of $7 9 . 6 5 ~ \%$

Electrochemical impedance spectroscopy (EIS) tests were conducted in $0 . 1 2 \mathrm { ~ M ~ C r C l _ { 3 } } + 0 . 1 4 \mathrm { ~ M ~ F e C l _ { 2 } } + 0 . 2 5 \mathrm { ~ M ~ H C }$ l solution. The amount of $\mathrm { H } _ { 2 }$ evolution during the electrochemical process was detected in situ using Differential Electrochemical Mass Spectrometry (DEMS,QAS 10OLi, Shanghai LinLu Co.,Ltd).The DEMS cell was a standard three-electrode configuration with a $5 \ \mathrm { m L }$ electrolytic cell containing $3 \mathrm { m L }$ of electrolyte $( 1 . 2 \mathrm { M C r C l _ { 3 } } + 1 . 4 \mathrm { M F e C l _ { 2 } } + 2 . 5 \mathrm { M H C l } )$ . The working electrode was a $1 \mathrm { c m } ^ { 2 }$ carbon cloth (pristine or modified),a platinum electrode served as the counter electrode,and an $\mathbf { A g / A g C l }$ electrode was used as the reference electrode.The electrochemical method employed was Cyclic Voltammetry (CV) with a voltage window of $^ { - 1 }$ to $_ { 0 \mathrm { ~ V ~ } }$ for the negative reaction.High-purity helium was used as the carrier gas at a constant flow rate of $2 ~ \mathrm { m L / m i n }$ .The $\mathrm { H } _ { 2 }$ signal $( m /$ $z = 2$ )was quantified based on the carrier gas flow rate and the ideal gas law, where $1 \mathrm { m L / m i n }$ corresponds to $4 . 4 6 \times 1 0 ^ { - 5 } \mathrm { m o l / m i n }$ . Thus, the $\mathrm { H } _ { 2 }$ evolution rate $\mathbf { \rho } ( \mathbf { y }$ -axis value in corresponding figures) was calculated as: Gas Concentration $\times$ Carrier Gas Flow Rate $\times ~ 4 . 4 6 \times 1 0 ^ { - 5 } \mathrm { ~ m o l / m i n }$ This setup allowed for the direct correlation of the electrochemical data with the real-time $\mathrm { H } _ { 2 }$ generation.

# 2.5.DFT simulation calculations

The DFT calculation in this paper is based on the plane wave technique in the VASP software package (version 6.3.2). The projectionenhanced plane wave (PAW pseudopotential) describes the ion-electron interaction. The generalized gradient approximation (GGA) represented by the Perdew-Burke-Ernzerhof (PBE) function is used to deal with the exchange correlation function.The cut-off energy of the plane wave base is $4 5 0 \mathrm { e V } $ In geometric optimization, the energy convergence condition is ${ 1 0 } ^ { \cdot 6 }$ ,and the force convergence condition is set to $0 . 0 5 \mathrm { e V } / $ A.The vacuum layer thickness is set to be $1 5 \mathrm { ~ \AA ~ }$ to avoid interlayer interaction.Integrating the Brillouin zone uses a $2 \times 2 \times 1$ grid of evenly spaced k points.In the initial CC model construction, $4 \times 3 \times 1$ supercells were selected to construct a graphene Oo1 surface containing 48 carbon atoms.The TCC model removes three carbon atoms and leaves the rest unchanged,furthermore,the NPCC model replaces two O atoms, one N atom,and one P atom on this basis. In order to obtain the geometric configuration of chromium chloridepentahydrate, we constructed a cube supercell with a length of $2 0 \mathring \mathrm { A }$ ,which contained one Cr atom,one Cl atom and five $_ \mathrm { H _ { 2 } O }$ molecules,and the adsorption energy was calculated as $\begin{array} { r } { \mathrm { E } _ { \mathrm { a d s } } = \mathrm { E } _ { \mathrm { t o t a l } } \mathrm { - } \mathrm { E } _ { \mathrm { s u b s t r a t e } } \mathrm { - } \mathrm { E } _ { \mathrm { c l u s t e r } } , } \end{array}$ where $\mathrm { E _ { t o t a l } }$ represents the energyof the total system,Esubstrate refers to the energy that adsorbs the surface of the substrate, and $\mathrm { \ E _ { c l u s t e r } }$ refers to the energy that adsorbs the substance.For the calculation of density of states (DOS),a denser grid of $4 \times 4 \times 2$ evenly spaced K points is used.We chose the CI-NEB method for the calculation of the transition state,and the visual effect was provided by VSTA.

# 3．Results and discussion

The fabrication process of the nitrogen-phosphorus co-doped carbon cloth (NP/CC) electrode is illustrated in Fig.la.SEM was used to observe the effect of the molten salt's composition on the carbon cloth surface.First,pristine carbon cloth shows a relatively smooth surface (Figs.1b and e).After heat treatment, the carbon cloth shows uniform micropores (Figs.lc and f),which can provide a larger specific surface area.(Wei et al., 202O) Fig.1(d and g) show that surface morphology of carbon cloth treated with $0 . 1 5 \mathrm { ~ M ~ }$ ADP.Compared with carbon cloth treated with other ADP contents, $0 . 1 5 ~ \mathrm { M - N P / C C }$ exhibits better uniformity (Fig.S1).after doping N and P the NP/CC retains similar morphology with CC and TCC,indicating that the N P-doping does not alter the structure of the pristine carbon cloth.In addition,the phosphate groups bring many oxygen-containing defect sites,which helps to improve the hydrophilicity of the carbon cloth.(Guo et al.,2024) Furthermore,N and P co-doping can introduce abundant active sites, promoting the adsorption of Cr ions and thereby boosting reaction kinetics.(Niu et al. (2O24)) Consequently, the NP/CC electrode is anticipated to enhance both mass transfer and the reaction kinetics of Cr ions. The EDS element mappings of NP/CC,as shown in (Figs.1h-1j),Nand P elements mani-fest coincident and uniform distribution on the outside shell,agreeing well with the SEM result.

The $\mathrm { N } _ { 2 }$ adsorption-desorption isotherms were tested to further explore the surface structure of the electrodes.As shown in Fig. S2,all samples exhibit type II isotherm,suggesting that thermal treatment and N,P doping did not alter the fundamental structure of the carbon cloth. Meanwhile, $0 . 1 5 \ \mathrm { M - N P / C C }$ displays a relatively larger pore volume (Fig.2a). It adequately proves that our presented strategy can significantly enlarge the surface area of primeval carbon cloth.

# 4．Conclusion

In summary,this work demonstrates a facile one-step strategy for constructing nitrogen and phosphorus co-doped carbon cloth electrodes for high-performance iron-chromium redox flow batteries.The optimized N,P co-doping introduced abundant defect sites and a modified electronic structure,resulting in significantly enhanced reaction kinetics and exceptional suppression of the hydrogen evolution reaction.DFT simulations verified that the N,P co-doped configuration strengthens the adsorption of the $\mathrm { C r } ( \mathrm { H } _ { 2 } \mathrm { O } ) _ { 5 } \mathrm { C l } ^ { 2 + }$ active species and elevates the energy barrier for $\mathrm { H } ^ { + }$ migration and $\mathrm { H } _ { 2 }$ formation,thereby effectively inhibiting HER.Consequently,in the absence of catalyst, the ICRFB employing the $0 . 1 5 ~ \mathrm { M - N P / C C }$ electrode possesses an EE of $7 7 \ \%$ when the operating current density was $2 0 0 \ \mathrm { m A } \ \mathrm { c m } ^ { - 2 }$ ,far surpassing the CC $( 6 5 . 7 1 \% )$ and TCC $( 6 8 . 8 \% )$ electrodes It was also able to operate stably for 300 cycles at $1 4 0 \mathrm { \ m A \ c m ^ { - 2 } }$ with a maximum EE of $8 3 . 4 7 \ \%$ and an average EE of up to $7 9 . 6 5 ~ \%$ .This study underscores the efficacy of multi-heteroatom co-doping via a simple synthesis route for developing high-performance,catalyst-free electrodes,presenting a promising path toward the commercial advancement of iron-chromium redox flow batteries.
