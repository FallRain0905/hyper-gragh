The utilization of intermittent clean energy requires high eficient energy storage technologies to minimize energy losses during charge-discharge processs.In this work,ionic covalent organic polymer (iCOP)composite membranes are presented to promote the battery efciencies of iron-chromium redox flow battry(ICRFB). iCOP powder was synthesized by interfacial polymerization method and the resulting composite membrane possessed superior physicochemical membrane.Sulfonic acid groups in the synthesized iCOP facilitated the proton transportation of the prepared membranes.A hydrogen bond network formed between perfluorinated PEM and iCOP further promoted the proton transportation of the membrane.Meanwhile,the positively charged amino groups under acidic conditions reduced the permeation of $\mathrm { F e } ^ { 3 + }$ and $\mathrm { C r } ^ { 3 + }$ ions due to the Donnan effect. Specifically, proton conductivity of iCOP-8 membrane reached $2 1 5 ~ \mathrm { m S ~ c m } ^ { - 1 }$ at $8 0 ~ ^ { \circ } \mathrm { C }$ ，Moreover,iCOP-8 demonstrated the lowest permeation rates for both $\mathrm { F e } ^ { 3 + } ( 1 . 4 4 \times 1 0 ^ { - 7 } \mathrm { c m } s ^ { - 1 } )$ and $\mathbf { C r ^ { 3 + } } ( 0 . 4 7 \times 1 0 ^ { - 7 } \mathbf { C m } s ^ { - 1 } )$ among all membranes herein.In terms of the ICRFB efficiencies,a Coulombic efficiency of $9 7 . 6 6 \%$ and energy efficiency of $8 7 . 1 1 \ \%$ was achieved at a current density of $1 0 0 \mathrm { \ m A \ c m ^ { - 2 } }$ The battery also operated stably for over 2O0 cycles without significant performance degradation (CE: $> 9 7 . 0 1 \ \%$ ,EE: ${ > } 8 6 . 0 1 \ \% )$ .

# 1． Introduction

Energy crisis caused by the ongoing consumption of fossil fuels has spurred the rapid development of renewable energy [1].Renewable energy such as wind energy,solar energy,hydropowerand geothermal energy has the characteristics of discontinuity and instability, posing significant challenges to the reliable and continuous supply of energy in large-scale power systems [2,3]. Thus,versatile energy storage system has been extensively employed to bridge the gap between intermittent renewable energy and continuous supply of energy in power grid [4]. Energy storage technologiesincluding lithium-ion batteries[5], sodium-ion batteries [6],supercapacitors [7],sodium-sulfur batteries [8] and redox flow batteries (RFBs) [9] have been substantially developed.Among them,RFBs possess the characteristics of high safety,long lifespan,flexibility in electrode and redox species design and low-cost electrolyte [1O-12]. In contrast to the medium and smal-scale energy storage technologies designed for portable devices and vehicles,most RFBs are well-suited to large scale energy storage,which is crucial for peak shaving and load balancing in power grids.

RFBs typically consist of electrodes,bipolar plates,collector plates and an ion-conducting membrane with two reservoirs storing electrolyte externally[13,14].The category of RFBsare normally classified by the redox-active species since the electrochemical reaction supplies the electrons to close the battery internal circuit[15]. Over more than fifty years since its first demonstration,a variety of RFBs including vanadium redox flow batteries (VFBs) [l6],all-iron redox flow batteries [17], zinc-based redox flow batteries (ZRFBs)[18],iron-chromium redox flow batteries (ICRFBs)）[19] and aqueous organic redox flow batteries (AORFBs） [2O-25] have been extensively investigated.As the first modern RFB announced by NASA in1973,ICRFB possesses the benefit of low redox species cost and environmentally friendly active substances, making it a promising option for cost-effective energy storage system

# [26,27].

As a key component in ICRFB,ion exchange membrane is pivotal in the isolation of redox active species while facilitating the transportation of charge-balanced ions within the battery.A variety of PEMs such as commercial Nafion [28],sulfonated poly (ether ketone) (SPEEK) [29] and sulfonated polybenzimidazole (SPBI) [3O] have been explored for the application in ICRFB.For instance,commercial fluorinated PEMs including Nafion 212,Nafion 115 and Nafion 117 were investigated to evaluate the influence of membrane thickness on ICRFB performance [28].Among them,Nafion 212 exhibited the highest electrolyte utilization (EU) and energy efficiency (EE) of $8 3 . 5 8 ~ \%$ at $8 0 \ \mathrm { \ m A \ c m ^ { - 2 } }$ ， ， clearly suggesting a relatively thinner membrane facilitated the performance of the battery．Additionally，non-fluorinated PEMsare emerging as competitive candidates for ICRFB due to their low cost and customizable chain structures[31]. Sun et al． synthesized a cost-effective SPEEK membrane with an optimized degree of sulfonation $( 5 5 ~ \% )$ ,achievinga Coulombic efficiency (CE) of $9 8 . 5 3 ~ \%$ and an EE of $7 9 . 1 3 ~ \%$ at $8 0 \ \mathrm { m A } \ \mathrm { c m } ^ { - 2 }$ [29].Likewise,a series of polybenzimidazole (PBI) membranes with varying degrees of sulfonation were synthesized for ICRFB with the electrostatic repulsion of imidazole aiding proton transport across the membrane while preventing the crossover of redox active ions [3O].The battery with S-PBI-1oO membrane delivered a comparable CE $( 9 8 . 2 ~ \% )$ and EE $( 8 3 . 1 7 \ \% )$ against all membranes investigated.

Recently,a notable increase in research on novel materials has been noticed in the development of high-performance PEMs.Specifically, researchers worldwide make great effort to explore the potential of composite membranes for the application in RFBs 32].For example,the incorporation of two-dimensional materials including metal organic framework (MOF) [33],graphene [34],carbon nanotube [35] and covalent organic framework (COF) [36] are all effective methods for the improvement of membrane performance.In detail, UIO-66 was grafted onto the main chain of PBI for the construction of ion transportation channels, resulting in low vanadium permeability and area resistance [33].The battery assembled with this membrane demonstrated 2000 stable cycles without obvious morphological and chemical changes in VFB.The incorporation of aminated carbon nanotubes into sulfonated polyether ether ketone (SPEK) also enhanced ICRFB performance [35]. The membrane with S/MWCNT- $\mathrm { { N H _ { 2 } } - 2 }$ exhibited higher CE $( 9 6 . 0 6 \% -$ $9 8 . 2 2 \%$ andEE( $8 2 . 0 4 \% - 8 8 . 0 9 \% )$ at a current density ranging from 40 to $1 4 0 \mathrm { \ m A \ c m ^ { - 2 } }$ compared with Nafion 212. Moreover,lower capacity decay $0 . 5 2 ~ \%$ per cycle) and longer self-discharge time of $^ { 1 8 \mathrm { ~ h ~ } }$ was observed in comparison with Nafion 212.Among these materials, COFs are particularly attractive due to their large specific surface area, tunable structures and excellent chemical stability [37-43]. More importantly,pore size of COF can be adjusted according to the specific applications, thereby achieving efficient molecular sieving and separation purpose. In detail, sNW-1 with an appropriate pore size $\left( 0 . 4 8 ~ \mathrm { n m } \right)$ was synthesized for accurate sieving of hydrated protons and vanadium ions in VFB [36].The corresponding membrane exhibited a CE of $9 8 . 7 \%$ and voltage efficiency (VE) of $9 4 . 8 ~ \%$ at $4 0 \mathrm { m A c m } ^ { - 2 }$ in VFB. Besides, a continuous SCOF/Nafion membrane with the wedge-tenon like reinforce structure was prepared for utilization in VFBs [44].The prepared membrane demonstrated a reduced pore size of $5 { - } 1 0 \mathrm { ~ \AA ~ }$ for effective sieving of $\mathrm { H ^ { + } / V ^ { n } \Sigma ^ { + } }$ ions,thereby enhancing the battery EE ( $8 5 . 5 ~ \%$ at $1 0 0 \mathrm { \ m A \ c m ^ { - 2 } } .$ ）and cycling performance $( > 9 0 0 \ \mathrm { h } )$ ). Recently， covalent organic polymers (COPs) based on irreversibly strong covalent bonds become attractive in the application of RFBs due to regulated ion transportation channel, excellent chemical stability and material flexibility [45,46]. The organic covalent bonds in COPs maintain unaltered structural integrity even in harsh chemical environments, thus providing a robust material as RFB membranes.Meanwhile,the formation of hydrogen bond networks in COPs also facilitates the ion conductivity in COP-based membranes [47-49]. In detail, Wang et al. prepared a self-supported COP membrane with sub-nanometer pore size of 4.5-6.4 $\mathring \mathrm { A }$ by a simple and effective in situ polymerization method using ireversible strong secondary amine bonding. Ultra-microporous channels were established with a continuous path for fast proton transport. With dual ion separation mechanisms of pore size sieving and Donnan effect, the assembled VFB achieved a CE of $9 9 . 0 \%$ and an EE of $8 3 . 0 \%$ at $2 0 0 \mathrm { \ m A \ c m ^ { - 2 } }$ [48].

In present work,iCOP composite membranes were developed to improve the battery eficiencies of ICRFB.The iCOP powder was synthesized through a Schiff-base reaction between 1,3,5-Triformylphloroglucinol (Tp)and $^ { 4 , 4 ^ { \prime } }$ -Diamino-3,3'-Biphenyldisulfonic Acid (DABDS). The structure of the synthesized iCOP powder was confirmed using Xraydiffraction (XRD),Brunauer-Emmett-Teller (BET),X-ray photoelectron spectrum (XPS),thermogravimetric analysis (TGA) and Fouriertransform infrared spectroscopy (FTIR). The physicochemical properties of the iCOP composite membranes including proton conductivity, mechanical strength,water uptake (WU),swelling ratio (SR), ion exchange capacity (IEC) and ion permeability tests were measured to evaluate membrane performance.The iCOP composite membranes were then assembled in ICRFB and the battery performance was assessed with respect to the influence of iCOP in the composite membrane.At last, battery self-discharge test,capacity decay and stability performance were presented to further validate the comprehensive performance of the composite membrane.

# 2.2.Synthesis of iCOP powder

iCOP powder was synthesized via the Schiff-base reaction [5O].In detail, $0 . 4 \ \mathrm { m m o l }$ Tp was dissolved in $4 0 ~ \mathrm { m L }$ of a mixture of n-octanoic acid and homotrimethylbenzene (volumetric ratio of 3:1).Meanwhile, $0 . 6 \mathrm { m m o l }$ DABDS and $0 . 9 \mathrm { m m o l }$ sodium formate was dissolved in $6 0 \mathrm { m L }$ of deionized water followed with ultrasonication for $6 0 \ \mathrm { m i n }$ .Subsequently, the aforementioned amine monomer solution was poured into the bottom of a $2 0 0 \mathrm { m L }$ beaker,followed by the slow addition of $4 0 ~ \mathrm { m L }$ of $\mathrm { T p }$ solution onto the top.The resulting mixture was left in static condition for $^ { 7 2 \mathrm { ~ h ~ } }$ .Afterwards,the upper solution was first poured off and then the as-synthesized powder was filtered with a sand core funnel (filter plate pore size: $1 6 { - } 3 0 ~ \mu \mathrm { m } )$ connected to a vacuum pump.The obtained material was then washed with DMF,methanol and water for1 h to remove any residues.Finally,the samples were dried in a vacuum oven at $8 0 ~ ^ { \circ } \mathrm { C }$ for $^ { 1 2 \mathrm { ~ h ~ } }$ to obtain the iCOP powder.

# 3．Results and discussion

# 3.1.Characterization of iCOP powder

The obtained iCOP powder was synthesized at room temperature, indicating the facile synthesis conditions and low energy consumption during synthesis process. The corresponding iCOP was initially characterized by XRD.As depicted in Fig.2a, two major crystal peaks at $3 . 2 ^ { ^ { \circ } }$ and $2 6 . 2 ^ { ^ { \circ } }$ were observed,corresponding to the diffraction peaks of (100) and (Oo1) crystal planes,respectively.These peaks aligned well with the simulations of the iCOP structure,clearly indicating that the synthesized material had crystallinity.To further evaluate the structure of iCOP, $\Nu _ { 2 }$ adsorption experiment was conducted.As presented in Fig.2b,the BET surface area of iCOP was found to be $1 0 . 1 \mathrm { \bar { m } } ^ { 2 } \mathrm { g } ^ { - 1 }$ , verifying the obtained material had less confined pores which was commonly observed in COF. Meanwhile,the BJH(Barrett-Joyner-Halenda) simulated pore size distribution map based on the $\Nu _ { 2 }$ adsorption isotherm indicated that the majority of pore sizes falls within the range of mesoporous materials. Such results can be rationalized by poor stacking between adjacent layers in iCOP [54,55].Furthermore,the FT-IR spectra was demonstrated to analyze the chemical composition of iCOP as depicted in Fig.2c.The characteristic peaks near $2 8 9 1 ~ \mathrm { c m } ^ { - 1 }$ were ascribed to the $\mathrm { C H } { = } 0$ stretch bands, proving the existence of carbonyl group in Tp.The characteristic peaks at $3 0 4 3 ~ \mathrm { c m } ^ { - 1 }$ and $3 1 4 7 ~ \mathrm { \ c m } ^ { - 1 }$ indicated the stretching vibration of $\mathrm { N - H }$ in DABDS.With respect to the FT-IR of as-synthesized iCOP,these bands decreased significantly while the presence of peaks at $1 5 7 8 \mathrm { c m } ^ { - 1 }$ and $1 2 8 9 \mathrm { c m } ^ { - 1 }$ were assigned to newly formed $\mathrm { c } { = } \mathrm { c }$ bands and C-N bands of iCOP,respectively, clearly suggesting the successful formation of iCOP as illustrated in Fig.1. Besides, the TGA curve of iCOP indicated that the main mass loss within the temperature range of $3 0 0 \ { ^ \circ } \mathbf { C } { - } 8 0 0 \ { ^ \circ } \mathbf { C }$ was attributed to the decomposition of sulfonic acid groups and the backbone of iCOP as showed in Fig.S2.These results suggested that iCOP was stable below ${ 3 0 0 ~ ^ { \circ } C } ,$ which was capable in the application in flow batteries.

# 3.3.Performance of ICRFB

In this section, the ICRFB performance of iCOP-x membranes were investigated.Initially,the electrolyte used in ICRFB was characterized bya standard three-electrode system.As illustrated in Fig.S6,redox peaks of $\mathrm { C r } ^ { 2 + } / \mathrm { C r } ^ { 3 + }$ ， $\mathrm { B i / B i } ^ { 3 + }$ and $\mathrm { F e } ^ { 2 + } / \mathrm { F e } ^ { 3 + }$ were all observed within the potential window between $- 0 . 8$ and $_ { 0 . 8 \mathrm { ~ V ~ } }$ vs. $\mathbf { A g / A g C l }$ .Moreover,a linear relationship $( \mathrm { R } ^ { 2 } > 0 . 9 6 )$ between ion redox peaks and the square root of scan rates was observed for both $\mathrm { C r } ^ { 2 + } / \mathrm { C r } ^ { 3 + }$ and $\mathrm { F e } ^ { 2 + } / \mathrm { F e } ^ { 3 + }$ redox couples,clearly suggesting a diffusional control of the redox reaction according to Randles-Sevcik equation.Next, battery performances with all membranes were carried out in ICRFB and the corresponding CE, VE and EE at a current density of $1 0 0 \mathrm { \ m A \ c m ^ { - 2 } }$ was compared in Fig. 7a. Among all membranes,iCOP-8 in ICRFB exhibited a CE of $9 7 . 6 6 \%$ and an EE of $8 7 . 1 1 ~ \%$ ，which was significantly higher than recast Nafion. Subsequently, the battery performances of iCOP $\mathbf { \nabla } \cdot \mathbf { X }$ membranes and the membrane with only monomer were given at current densities ranging from 60 to $1 8 0 \mathrm { m A c m } ^ { - 2 }$ as indicated in Fig. 7b-d and S7. Although such membrane enhanced the selectivity of the membrane, increased area resistance of membrane was demonstrated as both the VE and EE declined significantly.With respect to iCOP $\mathbf { \nabla } \cdot \mathbf { X }$ membranes,CE and EE of iCOP $\mathbf { \nabla } \cdot \mathbf { X }$ membranes were initially promoted with the increased iCOP content while an obvious decrease was observed when the weight ratio of iCOP increased to $1 \ \%$ . # 4.Conclusion

In conclusion,interfacial polymerization method was utilized for the synthesis of iCOP containing sulfonic acid groups and a corresponding composite membrane was proposed to enhance the battery efficiencies of ICRFB.The physicochemical properties of the composite membrane including proton conductivity,mechanical strength,ion selectivity, water uptake and swelling ratio were all significantly improved when the addition of iCOP was below $0 . 8 ~ \%$ in the composite membrane, verifying the promotion of proton transportation across the membrane with iCOP.The optimal mass ratio of iCOP was $0 . 8 ~ \%$ and the resulting iCOP-8 membrane showed superior battery performances,achieving a high CE of $9 7 . 6 6 ~ \%$ and EE of $8 7 . 1 1 ~ \%$ at a current density of $1 0 0 ~ \mathrm { { m A } }$ $\mathrm { c m } ^ { - 2 }$ .Moreover, such membrane shows a longer self-discharge time and lower discharge capacity decay compared with recast Nafion, suggesting that the addition of iCOP restrains the permeation of redox species across the membrane.In membrane stability test,the battery was stably operated for 200 cycles at $1 0 0 \mathrm { \ m A \ c m ^ { - 2 } }$ with competitive battery efficiencies (CE: $> 9 7 . 0 1 \ \%$ EE: ${ > } 8 6 . 0 1 \ \%$ ）while the battery with recast
