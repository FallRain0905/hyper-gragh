Sulfonated polybenzimidazole (SPBI) membranes have been widely applied in redox flow bateries (RFBs) for their excellent chemical stability and compatibility to a wide range of RFB chemistries.Nevertheles,few studies have systematically tailored copolymer monomer ratios to regulate ion transport for enhanced membrane performances.Herein,a series of sulfonated polybenzimidazole copolymers (SpNPBI-x) with varying monomer ratios were prepared for application in iron-chromium redox flow bateries (ICRFBs).The introduction of a certain amount of low-steric-hindrance monomers (terephthalic acid) reduces the channel size in SpNPBI $\mathbf { \nabla } \cdot \mathbf { X }$ membrane,whereas a higher proportion of the same monomers increases the free volume between polymer chains.In detail,the SpNPBI-0.1 membrane demonstrated a superior combination of high water uptake significantly exceeding that of Nafion 212 and dimensional stability (swelling ratio: $5 . 3 9 ~ \%$ ,attributable to its smallest ion transport channel among the SpNPBI- $\mathbf { \nabla } \cdot \mathbf { X }$ membranes investigated in this study $\mathbf { \boldsymbol { x } } = \mathbf { \boldsymbol { 0 } }$ ,0.1,0.2,0.5, 0.8).Due to the samecause,SpNPBI-0.1 membrane exhibited the lowest permeability to electroactive species in H-shape cel tests.Moreover,the ICRFB using SpNPBI-0.1 membrane achieved an outstanding Coulombic efficiency (CE) of $9 7 . 8 2 ~ \%$ and energy efficiency (EE) of $8 7 . 3 8 ~ \%$ at $1 0 0 \mathrm { \ m A \ c m ^ { - 2 } }$ ,exceeding battery efficiencies achieved with other monomer ratio membranes.Furthermore,the SpNPBI-0.1 membrane exhibited remarkable cycling life of 593 hours,demonstrating its extraordinary chemical stability in ICRFBs.

# 1．Introduction

Global warming caused by fossil fuels is driving the shift toward renewable energy sources [1-3].However, the large-scale utilization of renewable energy including solar energy,wind energy and tidal energy faces the challenges of intermittency and instability [4,5].Thus,the energy storage technology is critical to unlocking the full potential of renewable energy.Redox flow batteries (RFBs) store electricity from renewable energy as chemical energy and provide electricity as demand [6].RFBs have advantages of superior cycle stability, scalable capacity and high safety.As one of the first-generation RFBs,iron-chromium redox flow battery (ICRFB) has been developed for more than fifty years with the benefits of cost-effectiveness and high safety among all RFBs [7].However, the commercialization of ICRFB faces challnges including relatively low energy density and slow reaction kinetics.To address such efficiency limitations,the ICRFB performance was improved through the optimization of electrolytes，electrodes, ion-exchange membranes (IEMs)and operating conditions [8-14].For instance,Liu etal.modified the electrode with an electrocatalytic layer containing nitrogen-functional groups and $\mathrm { { B i } } _ { 2 } \mathrm { { O } } _ { 3 }$ ,enhancing the reaction activity of the redox couple [15].The ICRFB with the modified electrode achieved a superior energy efficiency (EE) of $8 1 . 7 8 \%$ $1 4 0 \mathrm { m A }$ $\mathrm { c m } ^ { - 2 } )$ ,significantly higher than that of the cell with original electrode. Besides,Li etal. chose guanidine hydrochloride as a negative electrolyte additive,boosting the reactivity of chromium redox couple [16].The corresponding EE of the cell with $\mathbf { 0 . 4 \ M }$ guanidine hydrochloride in the electrolyte surpassed that of the pristine electrolyte by $6 \%$ Zhou et al. developedamachine-learning-assistedapproachforefficient investigation of individual parameter impacts on battery efficiencies and further determined the optimal operation conditions [17].Such approach explored the impact of flow rate,channel spacing and current density on ICRFB and identified the optimal flow channel spacing. Meanwhile,the IEM serves as a core component in the ICRFB system, governing its key performance metrics,cycle life and overall cost [18]. At present,commercial Nafion 212 membrane is widely used in ICRFB. Although Nafion 212 membrane exhibits high proton conductivity,its microphase-separated structure aggravates the crossover of active species,impairing the battery efficiencies and ICRFB cycle life [19]. Moreover,the high cost and poor mechanical properties of Nafion hinder its utilization in large-scale energy storage commercialization [20].Consequently,such limitations have driven researchers to explore alternative membranes with the benefits of cost-effectiveness and environmental friendliness [21].

For ICRFBs,ideal ion exchange membranes require low membrane resistance and high ion selectivity.The widely used Nafion membranes generally exhibit poor performance in ICRFB,primarily due to the small ionic radii of iron and chromium hydrated ions.Non-fluorinated sulfonic acid membranes including sulfonated poly(ether ether ketone)(SPEEK), sulfonated polyimide (SPI) and sulfonated polybenzimidazole (SPBI) have been comprehensively studied recently for the application in ICRFB and many studies have demonstrated that the construction of well-developed ion channels is a viable method to achieve high ion selectivity in ICRFB.For instance,Bai etal. synthesized low-cost SPEEK-$\mathbf { X }$ membranes with different degrees of sulfonation (DS) and the SPEEK-57 $( \mathrm { D } \mathsf { S } - 5 7 \%$ )membrane exhibiteda CE of $9 8 . 2 8 ~ \%$ and an EE of 77.69 $\%$ $( 1 2 0 \mathrm { \ m A } \mathrm { \ c m } ^ { - 2 } )$ [22].Although SPEEK-57 membrane achieved low permeability,the relatively high resistance of the membrane resulted in a decrease of EE.Subsequently,the same research team fabricated a hybrid membrane by incorporating hydrophilic amino-functionalized multi-walled carbon nanotube $\mathrm { ( M W C N T - N H _ { 2 } ) }$ ）into SPEEK for the enhancement of ICRFB performance [23].At $1 0 0 ~ \mathrm { \ m A } ~ \mathrm { c m } ^ { - 2 }$ ，the S/MWCNT- $\mathrm { \cdot N H } _ { 2 ^ { - 2 } }$ membrane exhibited higher EE $( 8 2 . 0 4 ~ \% )$ than the

SPEEK-57 and Nafion 212 membranes.Furthermore,Zeng et al.also prepared a SPEEK-based composite membrane with zirconium dioxide $\left( \mathrm { Z r O _ { 2 } } \right)$ nanoparticles for ICRFB [24].The $\mathrm { Z r O _ { 2 } }$ -SPEEK-7.5 membrane exhibited a self-discharge time $( 5 6 . 5 \mathrm { ~ h } )$ ,longer than SPEEK-57 and Nafion 212. SPI is another non-fluorinated membrane widely utilized in vanadium flow batteries due to its low cost,excellent stability,low permeability and high proton conductivity [25-31]. Zhang et al.introduced a sulfonated Schiff base network (SSNW) covalent organic framework into SPI for the preparation of SPI-based composite membranes for ICRFBs [32].The synergistic efect of the hydrogen-bonding network between SPI and SSNW molecules and the size exclusion of SSNW prevented the penetrations of $\mathrm { F e } ^ { 3 + }$ and $\mathrm { C r ^ { 3 + } }$ . Consequently, the SPI/SSNW- $\mathbf { \nabla } \cdot \mathbf { X }$ membranes possessed lower $\mathrm { F e } ^ { 3 + }$ and ${ \mathrm { C r } } ^ { 3 + }$ permeability than those of Nafion 212 and pristine SPI membranes.Among all these non-fluorinated membranes,PBI is extensively used in fuel cels owing to its good stability and excellent mechanical performance [33-38]. Recently,growing interest in applying PBI-based membranes for ICRFBs has been spurred by the ability of protonated imidazole rings to repel $\mathsf { F e } ^ { 3 + }$ and ${ \mathrm { C r } } ^ { 3 + }$ in acidic electrolyte.For instance,Wang et al. prepared sulfonated polybenzimidazole membranes (S-PBI-x） with precisely controlled DS,combining high proton conductivity with low ionic permeabilities for $\mathrm { F e } ^ { 3 + }$ and $\mathrm { C r ^ { 3 + } }$ [39].The SPBI-100 membrane exhibited significantly lower permeabilities compared to Nafion 212 and achieved a high CE $( 9 8 . 0 2 ~ \% )$ and EE $( 8 3 . 1 7 ~ \% )$ $( 8 0 \mathrm { \ m A \ c m ^ { - 2 } } )$ in ICRFB. Our group developed a series of sulfonated naphthalene-based polybenzimidazole (SNPBI- $\mathbf { \nabla \cdot } \mathbf { x }$ ）membranes,in which the rigid naphthalene backbone ensured robust mechanical stability while the protonated imidazole rings leveraged the Donnan effect to enhance ionic selectivity [40].Among them, the SNPBI-1.42 membrane exhibited the lowest ion permeabilities and delivered excellent cell performance,achieving a CE of $9 7 . 5 5 ~ \%$ and an EE of $8 6 . 8 8 ~ \%$ （ $1 0 0 \mathrm { \ m A \ c m ^ { - 2 } }$ ).Recently,a growing number of recent studies have aimed at optimizing membrane performance by incorporating diverse monomers to capitalize on their respective advantages. For example,Wang et al. designed $\mathrm { P ( B F _ { x \% } { - } T P _ { y } }$ $\mathrm { \% ^ { - I m } } )$ copolymer membranes by strategically merging the benefits of hydrophilic dibenzofuran and rigid p-terphenyl, thereby attaining both excellent chemical stability and superior fuel cell performance [41]. Besides,Li etal. prepared SPEEK-co-PEK $\mathbf { x }$ copolymer membranes via copolymerization of hydrophilic SPEEK segments and hydrophobic PEK segments,which displayed significantly enhanced proton conductivity and excellent dimensional stability for fuel cells [42].

Herein,a series of polybenzimidazole copolymers,pNPBI-x,(where x represents the molar fraction of terephthalic acid monomer) was synthesized by adjusting molar ratios of terephthalic acid (TPA) to 1,4- naphthalenedicarboxylic acid (NDA).Afterward, the sulfonated membranes (SpNPBI-x) were prepared via direct sulfonation by sulfuric acid. The polymer structure and morphology of SpNPBI $\mathbf { \nabla } \cdot \mathbf { X }$ were characterized by FT-IR, $^ 1 \mathrm { H }$ NMR and SEM. The relationship between monomer ratio and polymer microstructure was investigated by molecular dynamics (MD)simulation,positron annihilation and X-ray diffraction (XRD). Furthermore,the membrane properties including water uptake (WU), swelling ratio (SR),mechanical properties,area resistance (AR) and ionic permeability were systematically compared.In addition, singlecell tests of ICRFB using SpNPBI- $\mathbf { \nabla } \cdot \mathbf { X }$ membranes were conducted for the comparison of CE,EE,voltage efficiency (VE) and cycle stability with Nafion 212.

# 4.Conclusion

In this work,sulfonated polybenzimidazole copolymer membranes (SpNPBI-x) were fabricated by varying the molar ratio of monomers (TPA and NDA). Systematic characterizations and ICRFB tests demonstrated that the monomer ratio effectively regulated the microstructure of membranes and promoted the battery performances.The narrowest ion transport pore width was observed in SpNPBI-O.1 within the investigated range,originating from its benzene components filling the gaps in the naphthalene ring $\pi \mathfrak { - } \pi$ stacking. Thus,SpNPBI-0.1 membrane exhibited the lowest permeability to $\bar { \mathrm { F e } } ^ { 3 + } ( 3 . 9 4 7 ^ { - } \times 1 0 ^ { - 6 } \mathrm { c m } ^ { 2 } s ^ { - 1 } )$ and $\mathrm { C r ^ { 3 + } }$ $( 3 . 7 2 0 \times 1 0 ^ { - 7 } \mathrm { \ c m } ^ { 2 } \ s ^ { - 1 } )$ among all SpNPBI-x membranes. The ICRFB assembled with SpNPBI-0.1 membrane possessed an outstanding EE of $8 7 . 3 8 \%$ at $1 0 0 \mathrm { \ m A c m ^ { - 2 } }$ ,surpassing that of Nafion 212.Moreover, the battery using SpNPBI-0.1 membrane demonstrated excellent longtermstability,with a self-discharge time of $2 5 . 7 3 \mathrm { h }$ (2.20 times to that of Nafion 212) and a cycling life of 593 hours.This study confirms that adjusting the monomer ratio of polybenzimidazole copolymers is an effective strategy to balance ion selectivity and proton conductivity. Future studies should tailor the microstructure of SpNPBI-x membranes by adjusting monomer ratios to match the ionic radii in different flow battery systems,thereby further exploiting their potential for energy storage.
