t gives us great pleasure to announce new annual“Energy Lectureship Awards” sponsored through a collaborative initiative between ACS Publications and the ACS Division of Energy and Fuels (ENFL). These Awards will recognize two talented investigators who have made distinguished research advances that address the world's clean energy challenges in a specific research topic area. Awardee selection will be conducted on a rotating basis by award committees formed at three ACS journals: ACS Energy Letters (2024),ACS Applied Energy Materials (2025)，and Energy & Fuels (2026) (Figure 1).

![](images/6e24f6c2ebd8a2d11e332e1f0549ca0d6a698ceddaf83887c68780db23af19ee.jpg)
Figure 1. The lectureship awards will rotate among ACS Energy Letters (2024),ACS Applied Energy Materials (2025),and Energy & Fuels (2026).

The specific topic for the 2024 Lectureship Awards is Energy Storage. The inaugural 2024 Energy Lectureship Award winners will be invited to present their research advances at a special ceremony sponsored by ACS Energy Letters and ENFL during the Fall 2024 National ACS Meeting in Denver, CO (https://www.acs.org/meetings/acs-meetings/ fall.html).

The two Award categories are distinguished as follows:

· Outstanding early career investigator conducting re-search in the area of energy storage. · Outstanding mid-career investigator conducting research in the area of energy storage.

“Early career researcher” is defined as a graduate student, post doc,or practicing scientist having no more than 1O years from receipt of Ph.D.“Mid-career researcher”is defined as a practicing scientist up to 20 years (but preferably between 10 and 20 years) from receipt of Ph.D.The nominees should have demonstrated outstanding research through their published work in energy storage, with a suggested emphasis on papers published in ACS Energy Letters,ACSApplied Energy Materials, and Energy & Fuels.Please refer to the ENFL Division Web site，https://enfl.aps.anl.gov/awards/energy-lectureship， for eligibility criteria and the nomination process for the 2024 Energy Lectureship Award in Energy Storage. The nomination deadline is February 29,2024.

We recognize the significant contributions made by our authors to the success of our journals,ACS Energy Letters,ACS Applied Energy Materials，and Energy & Fuels.This award provides an opportunity to recognize the contributions of researchers in our energy community. Please submit your nomination using the above link. The award committee is looking forward to your nomination.
