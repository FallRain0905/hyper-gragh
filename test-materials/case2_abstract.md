摘要：化工领域的实验知识天然具有多变量耦合特征，"体系—材料—条件—指标—机理"构成的高阶关系单元难以被传统的向量检索和二元知识图谱完整表达。为应对这一挑战，提出了一种面向化工文献的领域适配超图增强检索方法（Hyper-RAG）。该方法将化工实验知识建模为"实验事实单元"，通过超边同时关联多个异构变量，完整保留条件组合的语义信息。以液流电池和PFAS降解领域为案例，分别设计了领域本体并实现基于大语言模型的结构化抽取与超图自动构建，进而设计了向量数据库与超图数据库协同的双路径检索机制。以49篇液流电池文献为测试语料构建的知识库包含9970个实体和12987条超边（其中2715条为高阶超边）；以49篇PFAS降解文献构建的知识库包含7253个实体和10363条超边（其中2278条为高阶超边）。在涵盖两个案例共24个多层次问题的检索测试中，Hyper-RAG在液流电池案例中平均召回约103条超边和63个实体，在PFAS降解案例中平均召回约81条超边和61个实体，均显著优于传统Graph-RAG的召回水平。典型问题分析表明，Hyper-RAG在多条件约束、跨材料对比、跨策略比较、长短链PFAS差异分析和策略推荐等高复杂度查询场景中展现出更完整的上下文保留能力，其核心贡献在于通过高阶超边保留了"多个变量属于同一实验事实"的完整语义。

关键词：超图知识建模；检索增强生成；知识图谱；液流电池；PFAS降解；领域适配

**Abstract:**

Experimental knowledge in chemical engineering is inherently characterized by multivariate coupling, where high-order relational units composed of "system–material–condition–metric–mechanism" cannot be fully expressed by traditional vector retrieval or binary knowledge graphs. To address this challenge, a domain-adaptive hypergraph-enhanced retrieval method (Hyper-RAG) for chemical engineering literature is proposed. This method models chemical experimental knowledge as "experimental fact units" and employs hyperedges to simultaneously associate multiple heterogeneous variables, thereby preserving the semantic information of condition combinations in their entirety. Taking flow battery and PFAS degradation domains as case studies, domain ontologies were designed respectively, and structured extraction combined with automated hypergraph construction based on large language models was implemented. A dual-path retrieval mechanism leveraging vector database and hypergraph database collaboration was also developed. The knowledge base constructed from 49 flow battery papers comprises 9970 entities and 12 987 hyperedges (including 2715 high-order hyperedges), while the knowledge base from 49 PFAS degradation papers contains 7253 entities and 10 363 hyperedges (including 2278 high-order hyperedges). Across 24 multi-level retrieval test questions covering both case studies, Hyper-RAG achieved average recalls of approximately 103 hyperedges and 63 entities for the flow battery case, and approximately 81 hyperedges and 61 entities for the PFAS degradation case, both significantly outperforming traditional Graph-RAG. Typical question analyses demonstrate that Hyper-RAG exhibits superior capability in preserving complete contextual information for high-complexity queries involving multiple condition constraints, cross-material comparison, cross-strategy comparison, long-chain versus short-chain PFAS differentiation, and strategy recommendation. The core contribution lies in using high-order hyperedges to preserve the complete semantics that "multiple variables belong to the same experimental fact."

**Keywords:** hypergraph knowledge modeling; retrieval-augmented generation; knowledge graph; flow battery; PFAS degradation; domain adaptation
